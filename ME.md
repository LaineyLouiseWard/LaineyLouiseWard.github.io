# ABOUT ME
PhD student at University College Dublin in the School of Mathematics and Statistics, working on AI-driven approaches to weather and climate resilience as part of the national initiative Decarbonising Ireland: AI-Powered Pathways to Climate Resilience.

Previously, I worked as a Meteorological Officer at Met Éireann and hold an MSc in Atmosphere, Ocean and Climate from the University of Reading and a BSc in Physics from UCD. My MSc research quantified the UK’s need for underground hydrogen storage to achieve Net Zero by 2050 and identified the meteorological drivers of energy storage deficits. This work was recognised with the Met Office MSc Dissertation Prize for outstanding research.

My interests lie at the intersection of meteorology, energy systems, and AI—applying advanced methods to understand and mitigate the real-world impacts of extreme weather and climate change.

# ABOUT ISCHOLARS
Innovate for Ireland’s key objective is to attract world leading research and entrepreneurship talent to Ireland to conduct advanced, multidisciplinary PhD research to address global sustainability challenges.

A number of calls will operate under Innovate for Ireland. The first call, which is now open, is seeking applications from established Research Ireland awardees to rapidly create the first group of Innovate for Ireland iScholars – outstanding researchers with entrepreneurial qualities and a passion for sustainability. These iScholars will benefit from an increase in their stipend to a level of €28,000 for each year of their degree. Additional funding support for participation in the wider training and development parts of the Innovate for Ireland Programme will be provided at a later date (more information on these can be found below and in the call document).

iScholars will be the catalysts for navigating the transition to a sustainable future, through exceptional research, strategic vision, innovative mindset and the courage to challenge conventional practices.

iScholars will participate in a rigorous and exciting immersion into the worlds of entrepreneurship and innovation, in parallel to their doctoral research. Through immersive learning, real-world projects, and global collaboration, iScholars build the skills, mindset, and networks to shape what’s next. This is more than a qualification — it’s a launchpad. Graduates leave with leadership experience, a lifelong community of mentors and peers, and the confidence to create change wherever they go.
