I set up this blog to give postgrads a place to write informally about their research — short posts, no pressure. It started in the Decarb-AI Centre and I've since got the School of Mathematics & Statistics Postgraduate Committee involved to bring in more contributors from across the floor. I built and maintain the site, and I try to get writers involved in the publishing side of things too.
