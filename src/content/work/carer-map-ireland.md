Built in a single day at the Innovate for Ireland Ecosystem iScholars AI Build Day, a hands-on Claude Code workshop facilitated by Jonathan McCrea. I led a group that turned keyless open data into a working product: a vulnerability score for every neighbourhood in Ireland, mapped so charities, HSE planners and home-care providers can see where carer need concentrates and where services are thin on the ground.

The map reads by region, small area, or HSE area, and overlays where care services physically exist — so you can spot high-need areas with no support nearby. It's built on CSO Census 2022 and Pobal HP Deprivation 2022 data. The sister project, <a href="https://github.com/LaineyLouiseWard/the-indices-of-ireland" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Indices of Ireland</a>, is a worked example of the same pattern.
